\subsection{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Evolutionary dynamics as a nonlinear system.}
The formal setting is evolutionary game
dynamics~\citep{taylor1978ess,hofbauer1998evolutionary,hofbauer2003evolutionary}:
a population of competing designs is a nonlinear flow on a simplex, qualitative
changes of behaviour are bifurcations of that flow, and the objects of interest
are collective rather than
individual~\citep{perc2010coevolutionary,perc2017statphys}. That programme has
established that transitions between cooperative and defecting phases are sharp
and often reducible to a universal
form~\citep{perc2016phase,wang2015universal}, and that much of the outcome is
decided by who meets whom, through networks, layers or
conformity~\citep{santos2005scale,ohtsuki2006simple,wang2015multilayer,szolnoki2015conformity}.
Tags have been studied there as generators of assortment without memory, as
phenotypic similarity in well-mixed populations~\citep{antal2009phenotypic}
and as a mutating tag space in finite
ones~\citep{traulsen2007chromodynamics}. Two features separate this
system from that body of work. First, the tag is forgeable at a rate the
modeller sets, so the bifurcation parameter is a property of a verification
technology rather than of the players, and the comparative statics are with
respect to an engineering budget. Second, the flow carries two attractors
differing in whether the tag exists at all rather than in how much cooperation
it supports, so what matters is basin structure and not equilibrium level.
Whether either regime can arise at all turns on a classical condition: altruism
invades only under positive
assortment~\citep{eshel1982assortment,fletcher2009simple}, supplied here by one
provider's agents sharing a platform. By Proposition~\ref{prop:futility} the
badge contributes nothing to nucleation, so assortment is not one route among
several but the only one.

\paragraph{Greenbeards, forgeable tags and parochial conduct.}
Kin selection~\citep{hamilton1964genetical1,hamilton1964genetical2} explains
why a gene favours copies of itself in others, and a green beard is a gene
carrying both marker and
behaviour~\citep{dawkins1976selfish}. \citet{riolo2001evolution} showed that tag
similarity alone sustains cooperation without reciprocity,
and~\citet{roberts2002does} that this turns on cost, since a cheap tag is cheap
to fake, which the original authors disputed~\citep{riolo2002similarity}.
\citet{jansen2006altruism} showed that a continuously mutating tag space
outruns impostors, and~\citet{gardner2010greenbeards} reviewed the empirical
cases, which are real but
rare~\citep{keller1998selfish,queller2003single}. In biology, forgeability is a
fact about the organism; in an agent economy it is a fact about the
verification
infrastructure~\citep{jovanovic2024watermarkstealing,anbiaee2026threatmodeling},
so $\sigma$ is a policy variable. The closest structural ancestor
is~\citet{hammond2006ethnocentrism}, whose agents carry a tag and two
conditional strategies, our design triple minus the badge type. Their tags are
inherited faithfully and cannot be faked, so our question does not arise:
their result explains why differential treatment emerges, ours why it cannot
be removed from a certification regime without removing the regime.
\citet{choi2007parochial} pair in-group generosity with out-group hostility
too, but need group selection and explicit intergroup warfare,
while~\citet{masuda2015ingroup} give the standard condition that tag and
strategy be inherited as one unit, satisfied here by construction. We need
neither: one well-mixed pool with assortment, and nothing won by fighting.
Out-group harshness is favoured here because it is the only thing that makes a
costly membership signal worth acquiring honestly, so excludability, not
conflict, carries the result.

\paragraph{Signalling, certification and enforcement.}
In the handicap tradition signals are honest because they are differentially
costly to fake~\citep{zahavi1975mate,grafen1990biological}, and market
signalling separates types for the same reason~\citep{spence1973job}. Our
forger pays less than the compliant member, not more. Signal honesty need not
rest on a realised equilibrium cost~\citep{szamado2011cost}; in our model it
rests instead on detection. The setting is
nearer a market degraded by failed quality
signalling~\citep{akerlof1970market} and certification
intermediaries~\citep{lizzeri1999information,dranove2010quality}; the attribute
our badge certifies is never learned even after the encounter, which makes the
badge a credence rather than an experience
good~\citep{darby1973free,dulleck2006doctors}. Penalty and detection are
classically substitutes~\citep{becker1968crime};
Proposition~\ref{prop:fines} states the limits of that substitution when
detection is technological rather than budgetary.

\paragraph{AI races and agent identity.}
The interaction layer is the reduced race of~\citet{domingos2026falling},
descended from the development race of~\citet{armstrong2016racing} and
formalised evolutionarily by~\citet{han2020regulate}, whose programme covers
voluntary commitments, heterogeneity, auditing and
trust~\citep{han2022voluntary,cimpeanu2022heterogeneous,bova2024vigilant,bashir2026trust}.
Those instruments act on the race's payoffs or on an external monitor; ours
acts on who is recognised as an insider, which
Proposition~\ref{prop:reciprocity} shows is the only lever available in the
regime where the payoff levers have no purchase. Applied work on agent
governance treats visibility and provenance as
prerequisites~\citep{chan2024visibility,chan2025infrastructure,shavit2023practices,kolt2025governing},
and~\citet{hammond2025multiagent} name selection pressure and mis-identification
among multi-agent risks without formalising either. Work on model-origin and
counterpart cues documents self-favouring evaluation and identity-conditioned
interaction~\citep{panickssery2024llm,long2025mirror,wang2026outgroup}. Model
outputs carry detectable idiosyncratic signatures~\citep{sun2025idiosyncrasies},
but models show no general, consistent
self-recognition~\citep{davidson2024self}. We therefore treat recognition as
available but imperfect rather than free and reliable. What that strand lacks,
and we supply, is the population-level consequence: what an ecosystem
converges to when recognition is available, imperfect and worth faking.
