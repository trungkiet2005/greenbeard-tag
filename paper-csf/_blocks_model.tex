
\paragraph{The flow.}
Write $\Delta$ for the simplex of population states over the $48$ designs and
$x \in \Delta$ for a state. With assortment $r$, the expected private payoff of
design $i$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:fitness}
f_i(x) \;=\; r\,\piP(i,i) \;+\; (1-r)\sum_{j} x_j\, \piP(i,j),
\end{equation}
and the replicator flow is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:replicator}
\dot{x}_i \;=\; x_i\big[\,f_i(x) - \bar{f}(x)\,\big],
\qquad \bar{f}(x) \;=\; \sum_k x_k f_k(x).
\end{equation}
Every vertex $e_j$ of $\Delta$ is a rest point of
Equation~\eqref{eq:replicator} at every parameter value, and the eigenvalue of
the linearisation at $e_j$ transversal to that vertex in the direction of
design $i$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:eigen}
\lambda_{i \mid j} \;=\; f_i(e_j) - f_j(e_j)
  \;=\; r\,\piP(i,i) \;+\; (1-r)\,\piP(i,j) \;-\; \piP(j,j).
\end{equation}
The eigenvalue is affine in $\rho$, $\kappa_g$, $\kappa_f$, $L$ and $r$.
It is also affine in $\sigma$ unless both seats can carry a forged badge,
because the handshake of Equation~\eqref{eq:handshake} is bilinear in their
pass rates. This exception affects only the baseline mimic threshold: its
eigenvalue is $-0.2075\sigma^2+32.5075\sigma-30.300$ and has the unique
admissible root $\sigma_m=0.9377$. All thresholds below are therefore exact
roots of Equation~\eqref{eq:eigen}, not estimates.

On the edge $\{e_i,e_j\}$, the flow reduces to
$\dot{x}=x(1-x)(\alpha+\beta x)$ with
$\alpha=\lambda_{i\mid j}$. At a reported threshold the interior edge
equilibrium $-\alpha/\beta$ crosses the vertex and exchanges stability with
it, giving a transcritical bifurcation~\citep{strogatz2018nonlinear}.
The closed forms verify transversality, and $\beta\neq0$ at every reported
crossing; the numerical regularity checks are collected in the Supplementary
Material. Thus the table in Section~\ref{sec:results} is an inventory of local
edge bifurcations, while basin shares remain global numerical properties.
