\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The nonlinear analysis changes how certification should be evaluated. Local
edge bifurcations determine when a resident loses resistance, but they do not
select a unique global outcome: the same parameters support disjoint safe rest
faces and an unsafe mixed face reached from targeted starts. The
finite-population and replicator dynamics are therefore complementary rather
than competing descriptions. One measures resilience under recurrent
displacement; the other reveals what remains after a composition is left to
settle.

The first design implication is that an attestation standard cannot specify
verification independently of conduct. Current agent-identity proposals focus
on binding, checking and revoking credentials
~\citep{a2a2026spec,xu2026agentdid,grogan2025agentfacts,otsuka2026aiidentity}.
Equation~\eqref{eq:sigmastar} shows that in-group conduct sets forgery
tolerance, while Proposition~\ref{prop:exclusion} shows that out-group conduct
sets the cost imposed on an unverified but compliant entrant. Leaving conduct
to the market leaves the mechanism that sustains the credential unspecified
~\citep{hadfieldmenell2019incomplete}.

The second implication concerns measurement. Around the mimicry threshold,
unsafe frequency remains low while the posterior probability that a passed
badge is genuine collapses. Outcome monitoring therefore cannot distinguish a
working credential from a hollow one. A deployed regime must sample and
independently re-verify passed credentials; stronger interaction structure
makes this more important because assortment removes the observable exploiter
before the behavioural mimic. The supplementary provider experiment adds that
mutual recognition should be designed early: issuer-scoped marks convert
market concentration into a safety parameter, whereas federation removes the
category of cross-provider outsider.

These results are not a general argument against certification. Below the
reciprocity threshold, it is the only modelled instrument that protects safe
play, and under rare mutation it nearly halves unsafe frequency. The result is
more specific: its benefit is resilience rather than a lower settled harm
level, and its stabilising rent is an entry barrier rather than realised
equilibrium harm~\citep{leland1979quacks}. Asking a club to treat outsiders
gently removes that barrier but also makes honest membership dominated by an
unbadged or forged twin. A fine can restore resistance at fixed detection, but
cannot create a reason for the gentle club to exist.

The basin calculation also gives a methodological warning. Multistability is
common in evolutionary dynamics
~\citep{hofbauer1998evolutionary,hofbauer2003evolutionary,sandholm2010population}.
For any observable bilinear in population shares, scoring a basin-weighted
mean state creates cross-regime encounters absent from every trajectory. Basin
weights may average already evaluated observables; they must not be averaged
into a synthetic population first.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The interaction layer is a deliberately unchanged, two-player race with four
conduct rules. This isolates the identity mechanism but makes the conduct
space coarse; intermediate out-group policies could smooth the sharp frontier
in Table~\ref{tab:outgroup}. The directions implied by the invasion thresholds
should survive, but their numerical values need not.

Verification is represented by one exogenous Bernoulli pass rate shared by all
agents. Real systems include heterogeneous verifiers, revocation and
reputation carried across encounters~\citep{santos2018social,yue2025reputation}.
Adaptive forgers may also improve as detection improves
~\citep{jovanovic2024watermarkstealing}; endogenising $\sigma$ would turn the
static enforcement comparison into an arms race.

Assortment is a scalar self-encounter probability and therefore conflates
meeting the same design with meeting the same provider. A network of provider
communities would make the nucleation boundary depend on topology. Likewise,
neither rare mutation nor an undisturbed replicator rest point is a complete
model of a running market. An explicit perturbation rate would interpolate
between the two readings.

Finally, the uniform-start sample does not estimate the basin of the targeted
unsafe face. Its 64 perturbations establish only local numerical robustness,
so neither its global basin share nor the realised volume of cross-boundary
harm is known. The entry penalty is consequently an off-path invasion
quantity, not a prediction that mixed-boundary encounters occur with a
particular frequency.
