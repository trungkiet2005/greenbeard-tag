\begin{abstract}
A greenbeard is a signal that lets its bearers treat one another differently
from everyone else, and impostors can wear it. Machine agents now carry such
signals: provenance attestations and certification marks, cheap to check and
forgeable. We make the mark a strategic variable in a four-design race model,
holding the game fixed so every effect belongs to the identity layer, and
analyse the resulting 48-design replicator flow and its finite-population
counterpart. A design pairs a badge, genuine, forged or absent, with in-group
and out-group conduct; a forged badge passes with a fixed probability. Six of
the eight transitions are transversal eigenvalues crossing zero, so those
critical values are exact. Identity earns its keep only below a reciprocity
threshold, and below an assortment at which reciprocity holds a safe population
unaided. A club that reciprocates only within its membership tolerates more
than twice the forgery of one that trusts unconditionally, so in-group conduct
is a security parameter, and the protective fine diverges as forgery becomes
undetectable, so penalties cannot substitute for detection. Collapse is
graceful in the wrong way: above an assortment of about 0.08 a behavioural
mimic invades before the exploiter, hollowing out the credential while conduct
still looks sound, so better interaction structure leaves mimicry the only
failure mode. The flow settles onto one of two disjoint faces, badged or
unbadged, with nothing between them, so certification buys robustness rather
than a lower level of harm, held up by the pressure it puts on unbadged
conduct.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item A forgeable identity tag turns certification into a nonlinear population flow
\item In-group conduct is a security parameter: forgery tolerance 0.866 vs 0.400 at r=0
\item The protective fine diverges as forgery becomes undetectable
\item Behavioural mimics hollow out the tag before conduct ever degrades
\item Two disjoint attracting faces: certification buys robustness, not a safer level
\end{highlights}
