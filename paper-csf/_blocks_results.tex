
Table~\ref{tab:bifurcations} is a map of what follows. Each row is one
qualitative change in the flow of Equation~\eqref{eq:replicator}, together with
the parameter that drives it, the closed form that locates it and its value at
the baseline. The subsections then take them in order and say what each one
means for a certification regime.
